CHAPTER 1 / 18 · BLOCK A

為什麼生物資訊學需要生物學？

資料不是憑空冒出來的——每一個 FASTQ、VCF、count matrix 背後都對應到具體的生物現象、樣本處理與量測技術。理解這個對應，是不被資料表象誤導的關鍵。

Data doesn't appear from thin air — every FASTQ, VCF, or count matrix maps to a concrete biological phenomenon, sample workflow, and measurement technology. Understanding that mapping is the antidote to being fooled by data artifacts.

概覽

生物資訊學究竟在做什麼？

生物資訊學 (Bioinformatics) 並不是「生物學的程式設計版」，也不只是「處理生物資料的統計分析」。它的真正本質是：以計算與統計為工具，從生物量測產生的雜訊與訊號中提取有意義的生物學知識。

這個定義隱藏了一個關鍵假設——分析者必須知道「有意義」是什麼。沒有生物學背景的分析者，可能會把組織污染當成新發現的細胞型態、把批次效應當成疾病訊號、把粒線體 DNA 比對失敗當成「演算法 bug」。生物學知識決定了你能不能看出 P 值背後的真實故事。

Bioinformatics is not "biology done with code," nor is it merely "statistics on biological data." Its real essence is: using computation and statistics to extract meaningful biological knowledge from the noise-and-signal produced by biological measurements.

This definition hides a critical assumption — the analyst must know what "meaningful" looks like. Without biology, contamination becomes a novel cell type, batch effects become disease signal, and a mitochondrial-mapping failure becomes "an algorithm bug." Biological knowledge determines whether you see the real story behind the p-value.

💡
本章核心問題：當你拿到一個 RNA-seq count matrix，你看到的不是「12,000 個基因 × 8 個樣本的數字」，而是「8 隻小鼠的特定組織，經 X 試劑萃取 RNA、Y 套件建庫、Z 平台定序、W 工具比對所得的轉錄本豐度估計」。每一步都會在數字上留下痕跡——而你的工作是辨識生物訊號與技術假象。 The chapter's core question: When you receive an RNA-seq count matrix, you are not looking at "12,000 genes × 8 sample numbers." You are looking at "transcript abundance estimates from 8 mice tissues, RNA extracted with kit X, library prepared with kit Y, sequenced on platform Z, and aligned with tool W." Every step leaves a fingerprint on the numbers — your job is to separate biological signal from technical artifact.

量測的層級

生物量測的六大層級

分子生物學系統可從不同層級量測，每個層級都對應一類技術與一類資料格式。理解這個分層，是判斷「該用哪個資料解答哪個問題」的起點。

The molecular system can be measured at multiple layers, each tied to a class of technology and data format. Knowing which layer answers which question is fundamental.

🧬

Genome

DNA 序列本身：誰擁有什麼基因型？變異在哪？資料：FASTQ → BAM → VCF。

The DNA sequence itself: who has what genotype? Where are the variants? Data: FASTQ → BAM → VCF.

🎭

Epigenome

DNA 不變，但化學修飾與染色質狀態如何？資料：WGBS（甲基化）、ATAC/DNase-seq（可及性）、ChIP-seq（修飾位點）。

DNA stays the same — what are the chemical marks and chromatin states? Data: WGBS, ATAC/DNase-seq, ChIP-seq.

📜

Transcriptome

哪些基因被轉錄、轉錄多少？bulk RNA-seq、scRNA-seq、spatial transcriptomics、long-read isoform。

Which genes transcribed, and how much? Bulk RNA-seq, scRNA-seq, spatial transcriptomics, long-read isoforms.

⚖️

Proteome

實際的蛋白質豐度與修飾。LC-MS/MS（DDA/DIA）、TMT、affinity-MS、CITE-seq 表面蛋白。

Actual protein abundance and modifications. LC-MS/MS (DDA/DIA), TMT, affinity-MS, CITE-seq surface proteins.

🌡️

Metabolome / Lipidome

小分子代謝中間物與脂質。targeted/untargeted MS、HMDB / LIPID MAPS 註解。

Small-molecule metabolites and lipids. Targeted/untargeted MS with HMDB / LIPID MAPS annotation.

🔬

Phenome / Imaging

細胞形態、組織結構、臨床表型。bio-image（共軛焦/IHC/HCS）、digital pathology、EHR。

Cell morphology, tissue structure, clinical phenotype. Bio-imaging (confocal/IHC/HCS), digital pathology, EHR.

📘
快速回顧：中心法則是 DNA → RNA → Protein，但量測各層級的技術解析度、雜訊性質、可推論的問題完全不同。例如，蛋白質豐度與 mRNA 豐度的相關係數常在 0.4–0.6 之間，意味著「轉錄不代表蛋白」。 Quick review: The central dogma is DNA → RNA → Protein, but each layer has different resolution, noise structure, and answerable questions. For example, protein and mRNA abundances correlate only ~0.4–0.6 — "transcribed" ≠ "translated."
🚀
進階補充：從 CS/統計觀點，每個層級可視為「對同一隱變數 (cell state) 的不同 noisy projections」。多組學整合 (multi-omics integration) 的核心問題就是：在 sample/cell 維度對齊後，能否在 latent space 中還原出生物狀態？這條思路通向 MOFA、totalVI、Multigrate 等方法。 Deep dive: From a CS/stats lens, each layer is a noisy projection of the same latent (cell state). Multi-omics integration asks: after aligning sample/cell axes, can we recover the latent in shared space? This motivates methods like MOFA, totalVI, and Multigrate.

關鍵心智

生物資訊新手最常犯的五個生物學認知錯誤

1. 把「基因」當成單一實體

一個基因常有多個轉錄本（isoform）、多個編碼產物（protein isoforms）、不同細胞型態下不同表達模式。RNA-seq 量到的是 transcript / gene-level abundance，不是「蛋白的活性」。

One gene often has many transcripts, many protein isoforms, and different expression across cell types. RNA-seq quantifies transcript/gene abundance, not protein activity.

2. 混淆 mRNA 表達 = 蛋白質活性

蛋白質與 mRNA 的相關性常僅 0.4–0.6，更別說酵素活性、PTM 狀態、亞細胞定位都沒被 RNA-seq 捕捉。

mRNA–protein correlation is often 0.4–0.6; enzyme activity, PTM status, and subcellular localization are invisible to RNA-seq.

3. 忽略樣本來源與保存方式

FFPE、新鮮、冷凍、培養 → RNA 降解程度與 read 分佈完全不同。Tissue dissociation 偏好某些細胞型態，造成 scRNA-seq 細胞比例偏差。

FFPE vs fresh vs frozen vs cultured drastically changes RNA integrity. Tissue dissociation biases scRNA-seq toward easier-to-release cell types.

4. 忽視批次效應

不同日期、不同試劑批號、不同操作者都會在數據上留下系統性偏差，常常比生物差異還大。需要 ComBat / Harmony / mutual nearest neighbours 等方法校正。

Different days, lots, operators leave systematic biases often larger than the biology. Correct with ComBat / Harmony / MNN.

5. 把統計顯著當作生物意義

p < 0.05 不代表生物上有意義；effect size、生物學功能、可重現性才是核心。報告時同時呈現 effect、CI、FDR。

p < 0.05 ≠ biologically meaningful. Effect size, biological context, and reproducibility matter more — report effect, CI, and FDR together.
