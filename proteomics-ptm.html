蛋白質體與翻譯後修飾

RNA-seq 告訴你「轉錄了什麼」，但細胞真正做事的工具是蛋白。LC-MS/MS、DDA/DIA、TMT、PTM 富集——資料形態與 RNA-seq 完全不同，需要另一套統計與品管。

RNA-seq tells you what's transcribed, but proteins are what cells use. LC-MS/MS, DDA/DIA, TMT, PTM enrichment — data shape and stats differ entirely from RNA-seq.

原理

LC-MS/MS：把蛋白切成胜肽再量

標準 bottom-up 流程：(1) 萃取蛋白 → (2) tryptic digestion（trypsin 切 K/R 之後） → (3) LC 分離胜肽 → (4) ESI 離子化 → (5) MS1 全譜（質荷比 + 強度）→ (6) MS2 碎裂（HCD/CID）得到胜肽序列指紋 → (7) 資料庫搜尋配對胜肽（MaxQuant / Spectronaut / DIA-NN / MSFragger）→ (8) 蛋白組裝（peptide → protein）+ FDR 控制（PSM/peptide/protein 三層）。

Top-down 蛋白體：直接量整蛋白，可解析 proteoforms 與 PTM 組合；但通量低、需高解析儀器（Orbitrap / FTMS）。

Standard bottom-up: (1) protein extraction → (2) tryptic digestion (after K/R) → (3) LC separation → (4) ESI ionization → (5) MS1 (m/z + intensity) → (6) MS2 fragmentation (HCD/CID) for peptide fingerprint → (7) database search (MaxQuant, Spectronaut, DIA-NN, MSFragger) → (8) protein assembly + FDR control at PSM/peptide/protein levels.

Top-down: intact protein measurement, resolves proteoforms and PTM combinations — but low throughput, needs high-resolution instruments (Orbitrap / FTMS).

PTM

七大 PTM 與其專屬富集

Phosphorylation

Ser/Thr/Tyr 上，訊號傳導核心。富集：TiO₂、IMAC（Fe3+/Ti4+）。資料庫：PhosphoSitePlus。

On Ser/Thr/Tyr, signaling hub. Enrichment: TiO₂, IMAC. DB: PhosphoSitePlus.

Acetylation

Lys 上，染色質、代謝、訊號。富集：抗 acetyl-K 抗體 / Western blot。

On Lys, chromatin/metabolism/signaling. Enrichment: anti-acK antibody.

Methylation

Arg/Lys（me1/me2/me3）影響蛋白與 DNA 結合。富集：抗 methyl-K/R 抗體。

On Arg/Lys, alters interactions. Enrichment: anti-meK/R antibodies.

Ubiquitination

Lys 接 ubiquitin（K48/K63/K11 等不同 chain → 命運不同）。富集：抗 GG remnant（trypsin 切後 ubiquitin 留 GG）。

Lys-linked ubiquitin (K48/K63/K11 chains → different fates). Enrichment: anti-GG remnant after trypsin.

SUMOylation

類 ubiquitin，影響核 transport、DNA repair。富集：His-SUMO 純化。

Ubiquitin-like, affects nuclear transport, DNA repair. Enrichment: His-SUMO purification.

Glycosylation

N-linked（Asn）/ O-linked（Ser/Thr）、O-GlcNAc。富集：lectin、HILIC、PNGase F 處理；glycoproteomics 軟體：pGlyco3, Byonic, Glyco-Decipher。

N-linked (Asn) / O-linked (Ser/Thr), O-GlcNAc. Enrichment: lectin, HILIC, PNGase F; tools: pGlyco3, Byonic, Glyco-Decipher.
