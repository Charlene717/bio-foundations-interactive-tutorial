CHAPTER 2 / 18 · BLOCK A

細胞與胞器

細胞是生命的基本單位，也是大多數 bioinformatics 量測的基本單位。理解胞器的職責，能解釋 scRNA-seq 為何要算 %mt、為何 ER stress 會留下特定基因簽名、為何 nucleus-only sequencing 與整細胞測序結果不同。

Cells are the unit of life — and the unit of most bioinformatics measurements. Knowing what organelles do explains why scRNA-seq computes %mt, why ER stress leaves a signature, and why nucleus-only sequencing differs from whole-cell sequencing.

概覽

原核 vs 真核：兩種根本不同的細胞

細胞 (cell) 是所有生命的基本單位。分為兩大類：原核 (prokaryote) 與 真核 (eukaryote)。原核（細菌、古菌）無細胞核、DNA 直接懸浮在細胞質；真核（動植物、真菌、原生生物）有膜結構包覆的核以及多種胞器。

這個區別不只是「細菌簡單一點」——它直接決定了 RNA-seq、ChIP-seq、scRNA-seq 等技術的設計與分析假設。例如，原核轉錄與轉譯耦合（mRNA 還在被合成時 ribosome 已上去翻譯），這意味著原核 RNA-seq 不會有 splicing、5'cap、polyA tail，建庫策略也完全不同（如 RiboZero 移除 rRNA 而非 polyA 富集）。

The cell is the basic unit of life, in two classes: prokaryotes (bacteria, archaea — no nucleus, DNA free in cytoplasm) and eukaryotes (animals, plants, fungi, protists — membrane-bound nucleus and organelles).

This is not just "bacteria are simpler." It dictates how RNA-seq, ChIP-seq, scRNA-seq are designed. For example, prokaryotic transcription and translation are coupled — ribosomes attach while mRNA is still being synthesized. There is no splicing, 5' cap, or polyA tail, so library prep uses rRNA depletion (RiboZero) rather than polyA enrichment.

📘
快速回顧：真核細胞有「分區管理」——DNA 在核內、合成蛋白在 ER、能量在粒線體。這些區隔讓真核細胞能執行更複雜的調控，但也讓量測技術變得複雜（例如核漿與胞質的 RNA 分布是不同的）。 Quick review: Eukaryotes have spatial compartmentalization — DNA in nucleus, protein synthesis in ER, energy in mitochondria. This enables complex regulation but complicates measurement (e.g., nuclear vs cytoplasmic RNA pools differ).
胞器導覽

真核細胞的核心胞器與其資料學意涵

🧠 細胞核 (Nucleus)

功能：容納染色體、執行轉錄、執行剪接、做染色質調控。
資料學意義：snRNA-seq（單核 RNA-seq）只測核內 RNA，缺少胞質與粒線體 mRNA，與全細胞 scRNA-seq 在 marker 表達上會有系統差異。ATAC-seq、ChIP-seq、Hi-C 都針對核內染色質。

Role: Houses chromosomes, transcribes, splices, regulates chromatin.
Data: snRNA-seq (single-nucleus RNA-seq) misses cytoplasmic and mitochondrial mRNA — markers differ systematically from whole-cell scRNA-seq. ATAC, ChIP, Hi-C all target nuclear chromatin.

🔋 粒線體 (Mitochondria)

功能：氧化磷酸化、產生 ATP、控制細胞凋亡。擁有自己的 mtDNA（環狀，16.6 kb in human），編碼 13 個 OXPHOS 亞單位 + 2 個 rRNA + 22 個 tRNA。
資料學意義：每細胞數百到上千個 mtDNA，scRNA-seq 的 %mt 來自此。細胞破裂時胞質 mRNA 流失而 mtRNA 仍保留 → 高 %mt 是死細胞訊號（但心肌、肝、肌肉等高代謝組織例外）。

Role: Oxidative phosphorylation, ATP, apoptosis. Own mtDNA (circular, 16.6 kb human) encoding 13 OXPHOS subunits + 2 rRNAs + 22 tRNAs.
Data: Hundreds–thousands of mtDNA per cell. %mt in scRNA-seq comes from here. Ruptured cells lose cytoplasmic mRNA but retain mtRNA → high %mt signals dead cells (except in cardiomyocytes, hepatocytes, muscle).

🏭 內質網 (ER) 與 Golgi

功能：rough ER 上的 ribosomes 合成分泌型/膜蛋白；smooth ER 合成脂質、解毒。Golgi 加工糖基化、分選蛋白到不同胞器或分泌出去。
資料學意義：分泌型/膜蛋白在 proteomics 的偵測較低（疏水性、tryptic peptides 少），需 surface proteomics / O-glycoproteomics 特殊富集。ER stress → HSPA5/DDIT3/ATF4 基因簽名。

Role: Rough ER synthesizes secreted/membrane proteins; smooth ER makes lipids and detoxifies. Golgi handles glycosylation and sorts proteins.
Data: Secretory/membrane proteins are under-detected in MS (hydrophobic, few tryptic peptides) — needs surface or O-glycoproteomics enrichment. ER stress → HSPA5/DDIT3/ATF4 signature.

♻️ 溶酶體 / 自噬體

功能：分解蛋白、脂質、核酸；自噬清除受損胞器。
資料學意義：溶酶體儲積病（如 Gaucher）有特定基因簽名；mTOR-autophagy 通路與癌症、神經退化相關（檢驗 LC3、ATG 基因家族表達）。

Role: Degrade proteins, lipids, nucleic acids; autophagy clears damaged organelles.
Data: Lysosomal storage diseases have specific signatures (e.g., Gaucher); mTOR-autophagy is central in cancer and neurodegeneration (check LC3, ATG family expression).

🦴 細胞骨架 (Cytoskeleton)

功能：actin / microtubule / intermediate filament，提供形狀、運動、有絲分裂分離染色體。
資料學意義：細胞解離 (dissociation) 損傷 actin → 應激基因 (FOS, JUN, HSPs) 上調 → scRNA-seq 出現「dissociation-induced gene signature」假象，需要在分析時校正。

Role: Actin / microtubules / intermediate filaments — shape, motility, chromosome segregation.
Data: Dissociation damages actin → stress genes (FOS, JUN, HSPs) upregulate → "dissociation-induced signature" artifact in scRNA-seq, must be corrected.

🟢 葉綠體

功能：光合作用、固碳。擁有環狀 cpDNA（~130–160 kb），編碼光系統蛋白。
資料學意義：植物 RNA-seq 中葉綠體 RNA 比例（%cp）是品質指標，類似動物的 %mt。

Role: Photosynthesis, carbon fixation. Circular cpDNA (~130–160 kb) encodes photosystem proteins.
Data: In plant RNA-seq, %cp serves a similar QC role as %mt in animals.

🚀
進階補充：「胞器」對應到 cell biology 的概念抽象，但對資料分析者最直接的影響是「不同 RNA pool 的取樣偏差」。例如 polyA 富集 RNA-seq 偏好胞質成熟 mRNA、漏掉 nuclear-retained intronic transcripts；rRNA depletion 保留 pre-mRNA。snRNA-seq 偏向核 RNA、與 scRNA-seq 對 mitochondrial 基因檢測差異懸殊。在實驗設計階段就要決定。 Deep dive: "Organelle" maps to compartment-specific RNA pools that bias what your assay sees. PolyA-selected RNA-seq enriches mature cytoplasmic mRNA and misses nuclear-retained intronic transcripts; rRNA depletion keeps pre-mRNA. snRNA-seq sees the nuclear pool — mitochondrial gene detection diverges enormously from scRNA-seq. Decide at experimental-design time.

決策引導

scRNA-seq vs snRNA-seq vs spatial：怎麼選？

🌳 細胞層級量測決策

Q1:
樣本可以新鮮解離成單細胞嗎？→ 是 → scRNA-seq（解析度最高、捕獲胞質+核 RNA）。
Q2:
樣本是 FFPE 或難解離（如成人腦、心、骨骼肌）？→ 是 → snRNA-seq（單核），可避開解離損傷與細胞型態偏差。
Q3:
需要保留空間位置（病灶 microenvironment）？→ 是 → Visium / Slide-seq（barcoded array）或 Xenium / MERFISH（in-situ imaging）。
Q4:
要同時量蛋白？→ 是 → CITE-seq、TotalSeq 抗體偶聯 DNA barcode。
Q1:
Can sample be freshly dissociated? → Yes → scRNA-seq (highest resolution; cytoplasmic + nuclear RNA).
Q2:
FFPE or hard to dissociate (adult brain, heart, skeletal muscle)? → Yes → snRNA-seq — avoids dissociation damage and cell-type bias.
Q3:
Need spatial context (microenvironment)? → Yes → Visium / Slide-seq (barcoded array) or Xenium / MERFISH (in-situ imaging).
Q4:
Want protein too? → Yes → CITE-seq, TotalSeq antibody-DNA conjugates.
⚠️
常見陷阱：把 snRNA-seq 與 scRNA-seq 直接合併分析會造成 batch-like 偏差——核 RNA 富含未剪接 (intronic) reads、mitochondrial reads 極少。合併前需 batch correction，且某些 marker（如線粒體基因）不能比較。 Common pitfall: Naively merging snRNA-seq with scRNA-seq creates batch-like bias — nuclear RNA is enriched for unspliced (intronic) reads with minimal mitochondrial reads. Batch-correct first, and some markers (mt-genes) can't be compared.
實作銜接

從生物學到 scRNA-seq 的 %mt

# 為什麼 ^MT- ?
# Human mtDNA 編碼 13 個蛋白基因（命名如 MT-ND1, MT-CO1, MT-CYB...），
# 所有以 "MT-" 開頭。鼠類用 "^mt-"。植物葉綠體常用 "^cp-" 或 "^ChrC-"。
library(Seurat)
obj[["percent.mt"]] <- PercentageFeatureSet(obj, pattern = "^MT-")
obj[["percent.ribo"]] <- PercentageFeatureSet(obj, pattern = "^RP[SL]")
obj[["percent.hb"]]   <- PercentageFeatureSet(obj, pattern = "^HB[AB]")
# Apoptosis/stress signature score
obj <- AddModuleScore(obj, features = list(c("FOS","JUN","HSPA1A","HSP90AA1")), name="diss_stress")
import scanpy as sc
adata.var["mt"]    = adata.var_names.str.startswith("MT-")
adata.var["ribo"]  = adata.var_names.str.match("^RP[SL]")
adata.var["hb"]    = adata.var_names.str.match("^HB[AB]")
sc.pp.calculate_qc_metrics(adata, qc_vars=["mt","ribo","hb"], inplace=True)
sc.tl.score_genes(adata, gene_list=["FOS","JUN","HSPA1A","HSP90AA1"], score_name="diss_stress")
💡
記住：QC 閾值不是隨意設的——它源自於「胞器特性 + 細胞死亡的生物學」。心肌細胞 %mt 自然 20–40%，套用通用 5% 會把所有心肌過濾掉。 Remember: QC thresholds aren't arbitrary — they come from organelle biology and cell-death physics. Cardiomyocytes naturally have 20–40% mt; applying a 5% cutoff filters out all of them.
