CHAPTER 6 / 18 · BLOCK B

轉錄與 RNA 加工

從 DNA 到 mRNA 的旅程一點都不直接——啟動子、增強子、polymerase pausing、剪接、5'cap、polyA tail——每一步都會在 RNA-seq read 分布裡留下痕跡。

The DNA-to-mRNA trip is anything but direct — promoters, enhancers, pol pausing, splicing, capping, polyadenylation — every step leaves fingerprints in RNA-seq read distributions.

轉錄起始

RNA 聚合酶與啟動子

真核細胞有三種 RNA polymerase：Pol I（轉錄 rRNA，核仁內）、Pol II（轉錄 mRNA、多數 ncRNA）、Pol III（轉錄 tRNA、5S rRNA、U6）。

Pol II 啟動需要核心啟動子 (core promoter) 與通用轉錄因子 (TFIID, TFIIA-H) 組裝 pre-initiation complex (PIC)。核心啟動子元素包含 TATA box (–25)、Inr、DPE、CpG island、BRE。啟動子上游與下游的增強子 (enhancer) 透過染色質 looping 與啟動子互動，由特定 TF + Mediator + cohesin 介導。

許多基因 Pol II 在轉錄起始位點 (TSS) 下游 30–60 bp 處停頓 (promoter-proximal pausing)，由 NELF/DSIF 維持。P-TEFb 磷酸化 Pol II Ser2 後才釋放進入延伸期——這是另一層快速調控。

Eukaryotes have three RNA polymerases: Pol I (rRNA, nucleolus), Pol II (mRNA, most ncRNAs), Pol III (tRNA, 5S rRNA, U6).

Pol II initiation needs a core promoter + general TFs (TFIID, TFIIA-H) assembling the pre-initiation complex (PIC). Core promoter elements: TATA (–25), Inr, DPE, CpG island, BRE. Enhancers contact promoters via chromatin looping mediated by TFs + Mediator + cohesin.

For many genes Pol II pauses 30–60 bp downstream of TSS (NELF/DSIF), then P-TEFb phosphorylates Pol II Ser2 to release into elongation — an additional layer of rapid regulation.

📘
快速回顧：啟動子是「靠近 TSS、招募 PIC 的 DNA 序列」；增強子是「遠端、能加強轉錄、不依賴方向的調控元件」。它們的活躍狀態可由 chromatin marks 推測（H3K4me3 啟動子、H3K4me1+H3K27ac 增強子）。 Quick review: Promoters are TSS-proximal DNA recruiting the PIC; enhancers are distal, orientation-independent boosters. Their activity is read from chromatin marks (H3K4me3 = promoter; H3K4me1 + H3K27ac = enhancer).
🚀
進階補充：PRO-seq / GRO-seq 直接量到「正在延伸的 Pol II」，可看到 promoter-proximal pausing 與 enhancer RNA (eRNA)；RNA-seq 量穩定的成熟 RNA，不直接反映轉錄速率。在 ChIP-seq 中 Pol II Ser5-P 富集在啟動子、Ser2-P 富集在基因體內，是區分 paused/elongating 的指標。 Deep dive: PRO-seq / GRO-seq measure actively elongating Pol II — reveal promoter-proximal pausing and eRNAs. RNA-seq measures steady-state mature RNA, not transcription rate. In ChIP-seq, Pol II Ser5-P enriches at promoters and Ser2-P over gene bodies — markers of paused vs elongating Pol II.
RNA 加工

pre-mRNA 到 mRNA：三步加工

5' Cap 加帽

轉錄起始後 ~30 nt 即加上 m7G cap（7-methylguanosine 透過 5'-5' 三磷酸鍵連接）。功能：保護 mRNA 不被外切酶分解、招募 eIF4E 啟動轉譯。

m7G cap (7-methylguanosine via 5'-5' triphosphate) added ~30 nt after initiation. Protects from exonucleases and recruits eIF4E to initiate translation.

剪接 (Splicing)

由剪接體 (spliceosome, U1/U2/U4/U5/U6 snRNP) 辨識 5' splice site (GU)、branch point (A)、3' splice site (AG)、polypyrimidine tract，切除 intron 並接合 exon。

替代剪接 (alternative splicing)：exon skipping、alt 5'/3' splice site、intron retention、mutually exclusive exon、alt promoter、alt polyA——同一基因產生多個 isoforms。人類 90%+ 多 exon 基因會發生替代剪接。

The spliceosome (U1/U2/U4/U5/U6 snRNP) recognizes 5' splice site (GU), branch point (A), 3' splice site (AG), and polypyrimidine tract — excises introns and joins exons.

Alternative splicing: exon skipping, alt 5'/3' sites, intron retention, mutually exclusive exons, alt promoters, alt polyA — multiple isoforms from one gene. >90% of human multi-exon genes are alternatively spliced.

3' polyA 尾

CPSF 辨識 AAUAAA polyA signal（多在 stop codon 下游 10–30 bp），切割 pre-mRNA 並加上 ~200 個 A 的 polyA tail。功能：穩定 mRNA、促進核外運送與轉譯。

APA (Alternative PolyAdenylation)：許多基因有多個 polyA site → 不同 3'UTR 長度 → 影響 miRNA 結合與 mRNA 穩定性。3' Tag-seq、Quant-seq 等技術專門解析 APA。

CPSF recognizes AAUAAA polyA signal (10–30 bp downstream of stop), cleaves, and ~200 As are added. Stabilizes mRNA, drives export and translation.

APA (Alternative PolyAdenylation): many genes have multiple polyA sites → different 3'UTR lengths → impacts miRNA binding and mRNA stability. 3' Tag-seq / Quant-seq specifically resolve APA.

剪接事件

五大替代剪接事件

SE (Skipped Exon)

單一 exon 被跳過或包含，最常見類型。

One exon included or skipped — most common.

A5SS / A3SS

替代 5' 或 3' splice site，改變 exon 邊界。

Alternative 5' or 3' splice site — changes exon boundary.

MXE (Mutually Excl. Exon)

兩個 exon 互斥使用（A 或 B）。

Two exons used mutually exclusively.

RI (Retained Intron)

Intron 沒被剪掉，常導致 NMD 或保留調控元件。

Intron retained — often triggers NMD or keeps regulatory elements.

APA / Alt-promoter

改變起始或終止位置，影響 UTR 長度與 isoform 序列。

Alternate initiation or termination — affects UTR length and isoform sequence.

分析工具

rMATS（event）、SUPPA2、MAJIQ、Whippet（PSI 計算）；long-read：FLAIR、IsoQuant、StringTie2 LR。

rMATS / SUPPA2 / MAJIQ / Whippet (PSI); long-read: FLAIR / IsoQuant / StringTie2 LR.

💡
PSI（Percent Spliced In）：某 exon 被包含的比例，是替代剪接定量的標準。PSI 變化 ΔPSI > 0.1 且 FDR 顯著常作為事件 cutoff。 PSI (Percent Spliced In): the fraction of transcripts including a given exon — the standard splicing quantification. ΔPSI > 0.1 with significant FDR is a common cutoff.
