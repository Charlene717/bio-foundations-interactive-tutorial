Chapters 1–17 built the molecular-to-omics landscape. This chapter ties them together: multi-omics integration, genotype-to-phenotype, pharmacogenomics, FAIR & ethics — turning biology into clinical and scientific impact.

系統觀

從還原論到系統觀

20 世紀分子生物學是「逐個基因」的還原論——一個基因一個功能一篇論文。但複雜性狀（高血壓、糖尿病、思覺失調）背後沒有單一致病基因；癌症治療失敗常因「多通路冗餘」。

系統生物學把分子層級的網絡（基因、蛋白、代謝、訊號）視為整體：用 ODE 模型、Boolean networks、agent-based models、flux balance analysis (FBA) 描述動態行為。代表平台：CellDesigner、COPASI、PySB、Reactome、KEGG、HumanCyc、Recon3D（人類代謝模型）。

應用：合理化藥物組合、預測 KO 表型（CellOracle、scTenifoldNet）、優化發酵代謝（metabolic engineering）。

20th-century molecular biology was reductionist — one gene, one function, one paper. But complex traits (hypertension, diabetes, schizophrenia) have no single gene; cancer therapies often fail because of pathway redundancy.

Systems biology treats networks (gene, protein, metabolic, signaling) holistically — ODE models, Boolean networks, agent-based models, flux balance analysis (FBA). Platforms: CellDesigner, COPASI, PySB, Reactome, KEGG, HumanCyc, Recon3D (human metabolic model).

Applications: rational drug combinations, KO phenotype prediction (CellOracle, scTenifoldNet), metabolic engineering optimization.

多組學整合

跨層級整合的三大策略

🪡 早期整合

把所有 modality concat 後再做模型（PCA、autoencoder）。簡單但 modality 尺度與雜訊不同會主導結果。

Concatenate all modalities then model (PCA, autoencoder). Simple, but scale and noise differences dominate.

🪢 中期整合

每個 modality 學自己的 latent，再共享或對齊。MOFA+, totalVI, MultiVI, scMOMAT, Multigrate, scGLUE, GLUE 屬於此類。可學共享 + 私有 factor。

Each modality learns its own latent, then shares/aligns. MOFA+, totalVI, MultiVI, scMOMAT, Multigrate, scGLUE belong here. Learns shared + private factors.

🪜 晚期整合

各 modality 做完獨立分析後再融合（如 meta-analysis）。失去 cross-modality 互動訊號，但易實作。

Analyze each modality independently then fuse (meta-analysis style). Loses cross-modality interaction signal but easy to implement.

💡
選擇指南：樣本對齊（同細胞同樣本）→ 中期整合最強；非配對（不同樣本但同種 condition）→ scGLUE / GLUE 等用 graph + adversarial 對齊；只能 meta-analysis（公開資料） → 晚期整合。 Guide: Paired (same cell/sample) → intermediate integration. Unpaired (different samples, same condition) → graph + adversarial alignment (scGLUE / GLUE). Meta-analysis only (public data) → late integration.

FAIR 與倫理

資料分享與生物倫理

FAIR 原則 (Wilkinson 2016)：Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable。具體做法包括註冊到 GEO / SRA / EGA / ENA、提供 controlled vocabulary（HPO, MONDO, EFO, OBI）、使用標準格式（FASTQ, VCF, MAF, mzML）、提供 metadata。

關鍵倫理議題：

  • 知情同意：研究與商業使用界線、broad consent、re-contact 規範。
  • 隱私與再識別：即便去識別，匯總多個資料（如 23andMe 的姓氏推論）可重新辨識個體。基因組資料無法被「忘記」。
  • 族群代表性：歐裔過度代表使 PRS、藥物代謝預測在其他族群效果差，加劇健康不平等。
  • 意外發現 (incidental findings)：ACMG SF v3.2 列出 81 個 actionable genes 必須回報。
  • 編輯與生殖細胞：CRISPR germline editing 在多數國家受限（2018 He Jiankui 事件後）。

關鍵法規：HIPAA（美國醫療隱私）、GDPR + EHDS（歐盟）、GINA（美國基因歧視）、台灣個資法與人體研究法、HIPAA Privacy Rule 對 ChatGPT/AI 的 BAA 要求。

FAIR principles (Wilkinson 2016): Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable. Practically: register in GEO / SRA / EGA / ENA; use controlled vocabularies (HPO, MONDO, EFO, OBI); standard formats (FASTQ, VCF, MAF, mzML); rich metadata.

Key ethics issues:

  • Consent: research vs commercial boundaries, broad consent, re-contact policies.
  • Privacy & re-identification: even de-identified, combining sources (e.g. 23andMe surname inference) can re-identify individuals. Genomic data cannot be "forgotten."
  • Ancestry representation: European overrepresentation reduces PRS / pharmacogenomic accuracy for others, worsening health inequities.
  • Incidental findings: ACMG SF v3.2 lists 81 actionable genes for mandatory return.
  • Germline editing: CRISPR germline editing restricted in most countries since He Jiankui (2018).

Key regulations: HIPAA (US), GDPR + EHDS (EU), GINA (US, genetic discrimination), Taiwan PIPA & Human Subjects Research Act, HIPAA BAAs for ChatGPT/AI use in PHI.
