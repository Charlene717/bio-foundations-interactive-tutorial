突變與遺傳變異

「變異 (variant)」涵蓋從單一鹼基替換到整段染色體重排——尺寸、頻率、功能效應、germline vs somatic 等屬性決定了它們在 VCF 裡如何被記錄、用哪個工具偵測、如何解讀。

Variants range from single-base substitutions to chromosome-scale rearrangements. Size, frequency, function, and germline-vs-somatic origin shape how they appear in VCFs, which tool detects them, and how to interpret them.

快速回顧：SNV vs Mutation：在臨床/族群遺傳語境中，「variant」指任何序列差異（中性的也算）；「mutation」傾向指有害或新生變化。HGVS 命名規範可避免歧義（如 NM_004006.3:c.1521_1523delCTT (p.Phe508del)）。 Quick review: SNV vs mutation: in clinical/population context, "variant" means any sequence difference (including neutral); "mutation" suggests deleterious or de novo. Use HGVS naming for unambiguity (e.g. NM_004006.3:c.1521_1523delCTT (p.Phe508del)).
🚀
進階補充：每個 SV breakpoint 都應對應到具體生物學機制：NHEJ（NHEJ scar 短同源）、MMEJ（microhomology > 2 bp）、HR（gene conversion）、L1 retrotransposition（poly-A tail + TSD）、replication-mediated（FoSTeS / MMBIR）。breakpoint signature 反推機制是進階 SV 分析方向。 Deep dive: Each SV breakpoint maps to a mechanism: NHEJ (short homology scar), MMEJ (microhomology > 2 bp), HR (gene conversion), L1 retrotransposition (polyA + TSD), replication-mediated (FoSTeS / MMBIR). Breakpoint signatures back out mechanisms — an advanced SV-analysis direction.
功能影響

CDS 內變異的功能類型

Synonymous

不改變胺基酸（簡並性）；通常中性，但可能影響 codon usage、splicing、mRNA 穩定性。

Codon changes but amino acid unchanged. Usually neutral, but can affect codon usage, splicing, mRNA stability.

Missense

替換一個胺基酸（e.g. p.R273H in TP53）。影響由保守性、結構位置、PTM 推估，REVEL / AlphaMissense 可做 in-silico 預測。

Substitutes an amino acid (e.g. p.R273H in TP53). Impact predicted by conservation, structural location, PTM — REVEL / AlphaMissense for in-silico scoring.

Nonsense (stop-gain)

產生 premature stop，常導致 NMD（nonsense-mediated decay）→ 蛋白完全消失 (LoF)。

Premature stop, often triggers NMD → complete loss of function.

Frameshift

indel 非 3 倍數 → 改變讀框，下游胺基酸完全改變 → 通常 LoF。

Indel not multiple of 3 → frame shifted, downstream sequence changes → usually LoF.

Splice-site / splice-region

影響 5'SS、3'SS、branch point、polypyrimidine tract → 異常剪接 → 隱性 exon、intron retention。SpliceAI 是當前金標準預測。

Affects 5'SS, 3'SS, branch point, polypyrimidine tract → aberrant splicing, cryptic exon, intron retention. SpliceAI is current gold standard.

Regulatory

promoter / enhancer / 5'UTR uORF / 3'UTR miRNA-site 變異——多數 GWAS hits 在非編碼區，需 colocalization / fine-mapping / ENCODE cCRE 註解協助解讀。

Promoter / enhancer / 5'UTR uORF / 3'UTR miRNA-site changes. Most GWAS hits are non-coding — interpret with colocalization, fine-mapping, ENCODE cCRE annotation.

臨床判讀

ACMG/AMP 五級致病性

Pathogenic (P)

強證據支持致病。臨床可直接行動（如預防性手術、家系追蹤）。

Strong evidence; clinical action allowed (prophylactic surgery, family screening).

Likely Pathogenic (LP)

> 90% 概率致病。多數情境可比照 P 行動，但 caveat 較大。

> 90% probability pathogenic. Typically treated like P with appropriate caveats.

VUS (Uncertain)

證據不足以分類；臨床不能單獨據此行動。多達 60–80% 罕見變異落在此類，需 functional study 或家系數據釐清。

Insufficient evidence. Not actionable alone. Up to 60–80% of rare variants fall here — need functional / segregation studies.

Likely Benign (LB)

> 90% 機率良性。

> 90% probability benign.

Benign (B)

明顯良性，如高 MAF（> 5%）。

Clearly benign (e.g. MAF > 5%).

工具

VEP, ANNOVAR, SnpEff（註解）；REVEL/AlphaMissense（missense）；SpliceAI（splicing）；InterVar/PathoMan（自動 ACMG）。

VEP, ANNOVAR, SnpEff (annotation); REVEL/AlphaMissense (missense); SpliceAI (splicing); InterVar/PathoMan (auto ACMG).
