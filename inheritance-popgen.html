遺傳與群體遺傳

從孟德爾的豌豆到 UK Biobank 50 萬人——同樣的數學在現代 GWAS、PRS、admixture、選汰偵測中重複使用。理解這一層，能讓你看出資料背後的進化故事。

From Mendel's peas to the 500K UK Biobank — the same math underlies modern GWAS, PRS, admixture, and selection scans. Understanding this layer reveals the evolutionary story behind your data.

孟德爾遺傳

從豌豆到單基因疾病

孟德爾兩大定律：分離律 (segregation)——同源染色體上的等位基因在配子形成時分離；自由組合律 (independent assortment)——不同染色體上的等位基因獨立分離（僅當不連鎖時嚴格成立）。

單基因疾病遺傳模式：

  • 體染色體顯性 (AD)：每代出現、雜合即發病（HD, NF1, BRCA1/2）。
  • 體染色體隱性 (AR)：常需近親婚配；患者父母多為帶因者（CF, SCA, PKU）。
  • X-linked recessive：男性病多、女性主要為帶因者（DMD, hemophilia, color blindness）。
  • X-linked dominant：罕見；女性常較輕（如 Rett syndrome）。
  • Mitochondrial：母系遺傳，異質性 (heteroplasmy) 影響表現（MELAS, LHON）。
  • Imprinting：印記基因僅來自父或母的等位基因表達（Prader-Willi / Angelman, 15q11-q13）。

Mendel's two laws: segregation — homologous alleles separate during gamete formation; independent assortment — alleles on different chromosomes segregate independently (strictly true only when unlinked).

Monogenic inheritance patterns:

  • Autosomal dominant (AD): appears every generation; heterozygotes affected (HD, NF1, BRCA1/2).
  • Autosomal recessive (AR): often consanguineous; parents are carriers (CF, SCA, PKU).
  • X-linked recessive: males affected, females usually carriers (DMD, hemophilia, color blindness).
  • X-linked dominant: rare; females milder (Rett).
  • Mitochondrial: maternal inheritance, heteroplasmy modulates expression (MELAS, LHON).
  • Imprinting: expression from only one parent's allele (Prader-Willi / Angelman, 15q11-q13).
群體遺傳

Hardy-Weinberg、LD、Fst 與選汰

📐 Hardy-Weinberg 平衡

等位基因頻率 p, q 滿足 p² + 2pq + q² = 1 在隨機交配、無選汰、無突變、大族群下穩定。HWE 偏離是基因型分布異常的訊號（如 inbreeding、population structure、選汰、定序錯誤）。GWAS QC 必檢項。

Allele frequencies p, q satisfy p² + 2pq + q² = 1 under random mating, no selection/mutation, large population. HWE deviation signals inbreeding, structure, selection, or genotyping errors — a GWAS QC checkpoint.

🔗 連鎖不平衡 (LD)

兩個位點等位基因比預期更常一起出現。LD 由遺傳距離、重組率、族群歷史共同決定。GWAS 中 lead SNP 不一定是因果——常需 fine-mapping (SuSiE/CAVIAR) 找真實 causal variant。

Two loci's alleles co-occur more than expected. LD shaped by distance, recombination rate, and population history. In GWAS the lead SNP isn't always causal — fine-mapping (SuSiE, CAVIAR) finds the true causal variant.

🌍 族群分化 Fst

衡量兩族群間等位基因頻率差異程度（0 ~ 1）。Fst 高的位點可能歷經族群特異選汰（如 lactase persistence、SLC24A5 皮膚色素、EPAS1 高海拔）。

Measures allele-frequency difference between populations (0–1). High-Fst loci may reflect population-specific selection (lactase persistence, SLC24A5 pigmentation, EPAS1 high altitude).

📊 族群結構與 admixture

PCA / ADMIXTURE / STRUCTURE 將樣本投影到主成分或 K 個祖先成分上。GWAS 必須以 PC 校正以避免假陽性。Reference panels：1000 Genomes、HGDP、UK Biobank、PAGE、TopMed。

PCA / ADMIXTURE / STRUCTURE project samples onto PCs or K ancestry components. GWAS must include PCs to avoid spurious associations. Reference panels: 1000G, HGDP, UK Biobank, PAGE, TopMed.

GWAS 與 PRS

從關聯到風險預測

GWAS：在大型樣本（10K–1M+）中對每個 SNP 做表型關聯，顯著閾值 p < 5×10⁻⁸（Bonferroni for ~1M independent tests）。輸出 Manhattan plot 與 QQ plot。重要工具：PLINK、SAIGE、REGENIE、GCTA。

Heritability：表型變異中由遺傳解釋的比例。SNP-heritability h²_SNP 是 GWAS 能解釋的部分（多為總 h² 的一半）。LDSC、GREML 估計。

PRS (Polygenic Risk Score)：將 GWAS 結果加權求和成單一風險分數。PRS-CS / LDpred2 / PRS-CSx 等使用 LD 結構與貝氏縮放權重。限制：大多基於歐裔，跨族群泛化差；目前仍多為輔助、非獨立預測。

GWAS: per-SNP association in large cohorts (10K–1M+). Significance: p < 5×10⁻⁸ (Bonferroni for ~1M tests). Outputs Manhattan and QQ plots. Tools: PLINK, SAIGE, REGENIE, GCTA.

Heritability: the proportion of phenotype variance explained by genetics. SNP-heritability h²_SNP is what GWAS captures (often ~half total h²). Estimated by LDSC, GREML.

PRS: weighted sum of GWAS-derived effects. PRS-CS / LDpred2 / PRS-CSx use LD + Bayesian shrinkage. Limitation: mostly European-derived, poor cross-ancestry transferability; currently auxiliary.

模擬 HWE：拖動等位頻率 p，看基因型分布。

Simulate HWE — drag allele frequency p to update genotype distribution.

📝 自我檢測

1. 在大型族群中 SNP HWE p 值極小（HWE 不成立），最不可能的原因是？

1. In a large cohort, a SNP fails HWE strongly. The LEAST likely explanation?

A. 基因分型錯誤（如 cluster 失敗）A. Genotyping error (cluster failure)
B. 族群結構（subpopulations 混合）B. Population structure
C. 樣本太大造成功率過大（HWE 仍應成立但檢出小偏差）C. Large sample size with HWE still holding (only tiny deviation detected)
D. 強選汰D. Strong selection

2. GWAS 的全基因組顯著閾值 p < 5×10⁻⁸ 對應的修正方式是？

2. GWAS genome-wide significance p < 5×10⁻⁸ corresponds to which correction?

A. FDR 5%A. 5% FDR
B. Bonferroni 對約 100 萬個獨立檢驗B. Bonferroni for ~1 million independent tests
C. Holm 修正C. Holm correction
D. permutation pD. Permutation p

3. The main issue when applying a European-derived PRS to non-European individuals?

A. 完全沒問題A. No issue at all
B. PRS 直接無效（無相關性）B. PRS is entirely uncorrelated
C. LD 結構與等位頻率差異使預測力下降，可能加劇健康不平等C. Different LD and allele frequencies reduce predictive power, potentially worsening health inequities
D. 統計效能提升D. Increased statistical power
