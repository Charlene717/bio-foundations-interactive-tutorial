CHAPTER 12 / 18 · BLOCK C

表觀遺傳

同樣的 DNA、不同的細胞——差別在於染色質上「化學標籤」的組合。DNA 甲基化、組蛋白修飾、染色質可及性、非編碼 RNA、X 失活、印記，這些共同構成「表觀基因組」。

Same DNA, different cells — the difference lies in the chromatin "tags." DNA methylation, histone marks, accessibility, non-coding RNAs, X-inactivation, imprinting all make up the "epigenome."

DNA 甲基化

5mC：最熟悉的 epi-mark

哺乳類 5-methylcytosine 主要在 CpG dinucleotides。基因組整體 5mC 比例 ~70–80%（CpG），但 CpG islands（promoter 附近高 GC 富集區）大多為未甲基化狀態以維持轉錄活性。

三類規則：(1) Promoter CpG island 甲基化 → 抑制；(2) Gene body 甲基化 → 與活躍轉錄共存（與 splicing 相關）；(3) Enhancer 甲基化 → 多半失活。

動態調控：DNMT1（維持）、DNMT3A/3B（從頭）、TET1/2/3（5mC → 5hmC → 5fC → 5caC → DNA repair 移除）。胚胎發育與 PGC 發育中有兩次全基因組重編程。

Mammalian 5-methylcytosine (5mC) is mainly at CpG dinucleotides. ~70–80% of CpGs are methylated, but CpG islands (GC-rich regions near promoters) are mostly unmethylated to allow transcription.

Three rules: (1) Promoter CGI methylation → silencing; (2) Gene-body methylation → coexists with active transcription (linked to splicing); (3) Enhancer methylation → typically inactive.

Dynamic regulation: DNMT1 (maintenance), DNMT3A/3B (de novo), TET1/2/3 (5mC → 5hmC → 5fC → 5caC → repair-mediated removal). Two whole-genome reprogramming events during embryogenesis and PGC development.

組蛋白修飾

組蛋白語言

組蛋白 N 端「尾部」可被多種共價修飾：乙醯化 (ac)、甲基化 (me1/me2/me3)、磷酸化 (p)、泛素化 (ub)、SUMO 化。每個 mark 與染色質狀態強相關，組合形成「組蛋白語言」。

Histone N-terminal "tails" undergo multiple covalent modifications: acetylation (ac), methylation (me1/me2/me3), phosphorylation (p), ubiquitination (ub), SUMOylation. Each mark correlates with a chromatin state; combinations form the "histone code."

H3K4me3

活躍 promoter（TSS ±1 kb）。寫入：MLL/KMT2 家族。

Active promoter (TSS ±1 kb). Writer: MLL/KMT2 family.

H3K4me1 + H3K27ac

活躍 enhancer。前者單獨可標記 primed enhancer。

Active enhancer. H3K4me1 alone marks primed enhancers.

H3K36me3

Gene body 活躍轉錄區。Writer: SETD2。

Gene body of active transcription. Writer: SETD2.

H3K27me3

Polycomb 介導的facultative heterochromatin。Writer: EZH2 / PRC2。

Polycomb-mediated facultative heterochromatin. Writer: EZH2 / PRC2.

H3K9me3

Constitutive heterochromatin（重複序列、retroelement 沉默）。Writer: SUV39H, SETDB1。

Constitutive heterochromatin (repeats, retroelement silencing). Writer: SUV39H, SETDB1.

γH2AX (Ser139p)

DSB 反應指標——細胞遭到 DNA 斷裂後快速磷酸化 H2AX。

DSB response marker — rapid phosphorylation of H2AX upon DNA breaks.

特殊機制

X 失活、印記、跨代效應

❌ X-inactivation

女性兩條 X 中一條隨機（或 imprinted）失活。由 XIST lncRNA 包覆 cis X 並招募 PRC2/PRC1 → H3K27me3 / H2AK119ub。XIST 失調可造成 escape 與 X-linked 疾病表型差異。

One of two X's silenced (random or imprinted) in females. XIST lncRNA coats the cis X and recruits PRC2/PRC1 → H3K27me3 / H2AK119ub. Dysregulated XIST causes escape and varying X-linked disease severity.

🧬 印記

某些基因只表達父或母方的等位基因，由 ICR (imprinting control region) 上的甲基化決定。15q11-q13：Prader-Willi (父系缺失) vs Angelman (母系缺失)。11p15.5：BWS / Russell-Silver。

Some genes express only paternal or maternal allele, controlled by ICRs (imprinting control regions). 15q11-q13: Prader-Willi (paternal loss) vs Angelman (maternal loss). 11p15.5: BWS / Russell-Silver.

⏰ 表觀生物時鐘

Horvath 2013 多組織時鐘、Hannum 2013、PhenoAge、GrimAge、DunedinPACE——用 CpG 位點預測「生物學年齡」。與健康風險、死亡率關聯。可用 EWAS 探討環境暴露效應。

Horvath 2013 multi-tissue clock, Hannum 2013, PhenoAge, GrimAge, DunedinPACE — predict "biological age" from CpG sites. Correlate with health, mortality. Used in EWAS for environmental exposure.

🌱 跨代效應

環境（飢荒、毒物、壓力）能影響後代表觀，但人類資料尚未完全確立 epigenetic 跨代繼承（多數受混雜變因影響）。植物與 C. elegans 證據較強。

Environment (famine, toxins, stress) can affect offspring epigenome, but human transgenerational epigenetic inheritance is not fully established (confounded). Stronger evidence in plants and C. elegans.
