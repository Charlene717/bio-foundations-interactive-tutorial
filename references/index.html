教學註記

📌 教學註記與細節

下方為閱讀「生物資訊學的生物學基礎概論」18 章與本 reference 比對後，整理出的易誤解、近年共識更新與需特別澄清之處。原章節 Ch.5 已有 COSMIC v3.x 版本注記；以下為延伸主題。原教學檔案不修改，僅在此說明以利讀者參照。

The notes below collect points across the 18 chapters of the Biology Foundations Interactive Tutorial where the literature has moved on, where common misreadings recur, or where teaching shortcuts hide important nuance. Ch.5 already carries a COSMIC v3.x version note inline; the topics below extend that errata. The original tutorial files are not modified; the clarifications live here for cross-reference.

補充說明 · Notes

Ch.1, 2 · 三域 vs 兩域生命樹（Williams 2020）

原教學沿用 Woese 1977 / 1990 的三域樹（Bacteria、Archaea、Eukarya 為三個並列分支；Woese et al. PNAS 87:4576, 1990, DOI）。但 2010 年代後系統發生學新證據強烈支持 “two-domain tree” / Eocyte 假說：真核生物並非與古菌並列，而是從古菌 Asgard 超門內演化而來（特別是 Heimdallarchaeota / Lokiarchaeota；Spang et al., Nature 521:173, 2015, DOI；Williams et al., Nat Ecol Evol 4:138, 2020, DOI；Imachi et al., Nature 577:519, 2020 — 首次培養 Asgard 古菌 Prometheoarchaeum, DOI）。教學中可保留 Woese 三域作為歷史里程碑，但應同時呈現 2020 後的兩域共識，並指出粒線體源自 α-變形菌（endosymbiosis）獨立於宿主古菌譜系。

The tutorial uses the Woese 1977/1990 three-domain tree (Bacteria, Archaea, Eukarya as three sister branches; Woese et al. PNAS 87:4576, 1990, DOI). Post-2010 phylogenomic evidence strongly supports a two-domain tree / eocyte hypothesis: eukaryotes are not a sister to Archaea but arose from within the archaeal Asgard superphylum (especially Heimdallarchaeota / Lokiarchaeota; Spang et al., Nature 521:173, 2015, DOI; Williams et al., Nat Ecol Evol 4:138, 2020, DOI; Imachi et al., Nature 577:519, 2020 — first cultured Asgard archaeon Prometheoarchaeum, DOI). Keep Woese as a historical milestone but also present the post-2020 two-domain consensus, noting that mitochondria came from an α-proteobacterial endosymbiosis independent of the host archaeal lineage.

補充說明 · Notes

Ch.9 · 「Junk DNA」與 ENCODE 2012 爭議（Doolittle 2013）

原教學引用 ENCODE 2012 的著名結論「人類基因組 80% 具有功能」（ENCODE Project Consortium, Nature 489:57, 2012, DOI），但該結論在學界引發強烈爭議至今未平息，學生不應將 80% 視為共識。批評主要集中在「功能」(function) 的定義過寬：(1) Graur et al. Genome Biol Evol 5:578, 2013（DOI，標題為 "On the immortality of television sets"）論證 ENCODE 採用「causal-role 功能」(任何被轉錄、結合 TF 或 modified histone 的位置皆視為功能) 而非「selected-effect 功能」(在演化上承受選擇壓力者)；(2) Doolittle 2013, PNAS 110:5294（DOI）強調比較基因組學顯示僅約 8–10% 的人類基因組顯示選擇保守訊號，與 80% 數字差距巨大；(3) Rands et al. PLOS Genet 10:e1004525, 2014（DOI）給出 ~8.2% 的限制性 (constrained) 估計。共識：「junk DNA」一詞雖過於簡化（許多重複序列、ERV、enhancer 有功能），但用「80% functional」描述整個基因組仍是錯誤。教學中應同時呈現 ENCODE 的生化活動圖譜與演化保守度兩個正交軸。

The tutorial cites the well-known ENCODE 2012 claim that "80% of the human genome is functional" (ENCODE Project Consortium, Nature 489:57, 2012, DOI), but this claim remains contested and students should not treat 80% as consensus. Criticisms target ENCODE's overly broad definition of "function": (1) Graur et al. Genome Biol Evol 5:578, 2013 (DOI, titled "On the immortality of television sets") argue ENCODE used a "causal-role function" (any site that is transcribed, TF-bound, or has modified histones counts) rather than a "selected-effect function" (positions under purifying selection); (2) Doolittle 2013, PNAS 110:5294 (DOI) emphasises comparative genomics showing only ~8–10% of the human genome carries a signal of evolutionary constraint, far below 80%; (3) Rands et al. PLOS Genet 10:e1004525, 2014 (DOI) give ~8.2% as the constrained fraction. Consensus: "junk DNA" is an oversimplification (many repeats, ERVs, and enhancers are functional), but describing the whole genome as "80% functional" is still wrong. Present ENCODE's biochemical-activity map and evolutionary conservation as two orthogonal axes.

Ch.11, 12 · Lamarck 透過表觀遺傳的「部分平反」（Heard & Martienssen 2014）

原教學在遺傳學章節將 Lamarck 的「獲得性性狀遺傳」描述為已被達爾文 / Weismann 屏障（germline / soma separation, Weismann 1893）徹底駁倒。這在個體獲得的形態變化不傳給後代這點上仍正確，但表觀遺傳學在過去 20 年提供了 Lamarck 部分平反的證據。Heard & Martienssen 2014, Cell 157:95（DOI，題為 "Transgenerational epigenetic inheritance: myths and mechanisms"）系統性審查了哺乳動物與植物的跨代表觀遺傳：(1) 在 線蟲（C. elegans，piRNA-mediated）與植物（DNA methylation, paramutation）跨代傳遞證據強；(2) 在哺乳動物證據較弱但存在 — 荷蘭飢餓冬（Dutch Hunger Winter 1944-45）的孫代代謝表型研究（Heijmans et al., PNAS 105:17046, 2008, DOI），以及小鼠應激後 sperm tsRNA 傳遞代謝 / 行為表型（Chen et al., Science 351:397, 2016, DOI）；(3) 但哺乳動物受精後的表觀重編程（global demethylation）強烈削弱大多數標記，所以宣稱完整 Lamarckian inheritance 仍須謹慎。教學中應同時呈現 Weismann 屏障的主流地位與表觀遺傳的「窗口」現象。

The genetics chapter portrays Lamarck's inheritance of acquired traits as definitively refuted by Darwin / the Weismann barrier (germline–soma separation, Weismann 1893). This remains correct in the sense that individually acquired morphological changes are not transmitted, but epigenetics has partially rehabilitated Lamarck over the last 20 years. Heard & Martienssen 2014, Cell 157:95 (DOI, "Transgenerational epigenetic inheritance: myths and mechanisms") systematically reviewed mammalian and plant transgenerational epigenetics: (1) evidence is strong in C. elegans (piRNA-mediated) and plants (DNA methylation, paramutation); (2) evidence in mammals is weaker but real — grand-offspring metabolic phenotypes in the Dutch Hunger Winter cohort (Heijmans et al., PNAS 105:17046, 2008, DOI), and stress-induced sperm tsRNA transmitting metabolic/behavioural traits in mice (Chen et al., Science 351:397, 2016, DOI); (3) but mammalian post-fertilisation global demethylation strongly erases most marks, so claims of full Lamarckian inheritance need caution. Present both the dominant Weismann barrier and the narrow "epigenetic window" together.

Ch.7 · IRES 與 cap-independent translation（Pelletier & Sonenberg 1988）

原教學在 translation 章節描述真核生物轉譯為「cap-dependent scanning」，從 5′ m7G cap → 40S 招募 → AUG 啟動。這在管家基因下確實佔絕大多數，但遺漏了 cap-independent translation 的重要機制。Pelletier & Sonenberg 1988, Nature 334:320（DOI）首次在小兒麻痺病毒 RNA 上鑑定 IRES (Internal Ribosome Entry Site)，使核糖體可直接內部結合 RNA 結構而不需 cap。後續發現：(1) 許多病毒（picornavirus、HCV、SARS-CoV-2 的 sgRNA）仰賴 IRES；(2) 細胞 IRES 在 cap-dependent 翻譯受抑制條件下（應激、凋亡、有絲分裂）驅動關鍵基因表達，例如 XIAP、c-Myc、Bcl-xL、HIF1α、VEGF（Komar & Hatzoglou, Cell Cycle 10:229, 2011, DOI）；(3) m6A-mediated cap-independent translation 是另一條路徑（Meyer et al., Cell 163:999, 2015, DOI），m6A 在 5′UTR 可招募 eIF3 直接啟動翻譯。教學中應指出 cap-dependent 是常態，但 IRES / m6A-driven 翻譯在應激、發育與病毒感染時極為關鍵。

The translation chapter describes eukaryotic initiation as cap-dependent scanning: 5′ m7G cap → 40S recruitment → AUG start. This is the housekeeping default but omits cap-independent translation. Pelletier & Sonenberg 1988, Nature 334:320 (DOI) first identified the IRES (Internal Ribosome Entry Site) in poliovirus RNA, allowing direct ribosome binding to an internal RNA structure without a cap. Since then: (1) many viruses (picornaviruses, HCV, SARS-CoV-2 sgRNAs) depend on IRES elements; (2) cellular IRESes drive expression of key genes when cap-dependent translation is suppressed (stress, apoptosis, mitosis) — e.g., XIAP, c-Myc, Bcl-xL, HIF1α, VEGF (Komar & Hatzoglou, Cell Cycle 10:229, 2011, DOI); (3) m6A-mediated cap-independent translation is another route (Meyer et al., Cell 163:999, 2015, DOI), where 5′UTR m6A recruits eIF3 directly. Teach cap-dependent scanning as the default, but flag IRES / m6A-driven translation as critical during stress, development, and viral infection.

Ch.13 · 細胞死亡：apoptosis vs necroptosis（Galluzzi 2018 NCCD）

原教學在細胞週期 / 訊息章節主要介紹兩種細胞死亡：apoptosis（程序性、caspase-依賴、無發炎）與 necrosis（被動、發炎性）。這個 1980 年代的二分法已被 NCCD 2018 全面更新。Galluzzi et al.（Nomenclature Committee on Cell Death）2018, Cell Death Differ 25:486（DOI）定義 13 種以上 regulated cell death 類型，其中與教學相關的關鍵增補：(1) Necroptosis = 程序性壞死，由 RIPK1 / RIPK3 / MLKL 介導，雖如壞死般造成膜破裂與發炎，但是受嚴格調控的（Sun et al., Cell 148:213, 2012, DOI）；(2) Pyroptosis（gasdermin-mediated，inflammasome 下游）；(3) Ferroptosis（鐵依賴性脂質過氧化，GPX4 喪失誘導，Dixon et al., Cell 149:1060, 2012, DOI）；(4) NETosis（嗜中性球外網釋放）。臨床意義：抗腫瘤免疫療法積極利用 immunogenic cell death（pyroptosis、necroptosis、ferroptosis）刺激抗腫瘤反應，而不是傳統 apoptosis（後者本意是「沉默」死亡）。教學中應以 NCCD 2018 取代過時的「apoptosis vs necrosis」二分法。

The cell-cycle / signalling chapter mainly contrasts apoptosis (programmed, caspase-dependent, non-inflammatory) with necrosis (passive, inflammatory). This 1980s dichotomy has been thoroughly updated by NCCD 2018. Galluzzi et al. (Nomenclature Committee on Cell Death) 2018, Cell Death Differ 25:486 (DOI) defines 13+ regulated cell death modalities; the key additions for teaching are: (1) Necroptosis = programmed necrosis, mediated by RIPK1 / RIPK3 / MLKL, ruptures membranes and triggers inflammation like necrosis but is tightly regulated (Sun et al., Cell 148:213, 2012, DOI); (2) Pyroptosis (gasdermin-mediated, downstream of inflammasomes); (3) Ferroptosis (iron-dependent lipid peroxidation, induced by GPX4 loss, Dixon et al., Cell 149:1060, 2012, DOI); (4) NETosis (neutrophil extracellular trap release). Clinical relevance: anti-tumour immunotherapy actively exploits immunogenic cell death (pyroptosis, necroptosis, ferroptosis) to drive anti-tumour responses, whereas classical apoptosis was designed to be "silent." Replace the obsolete apoptosis-vs-necrosis dichotomy with NCCD 2018.

Ch.11 · mtDNA 父系滲漏（paternal leakage）— Luo 2018 PNAS 的爭議與重新評估

原教學在遺傳學章節述及「粒線體 DNA 嚴格母系遺傳」並以此作為人類粒線體 Eve 與母系族譜研究的基礎。這在絕大多數情況下成立，但並非絕對。Luo et al. 2018, PNAS 115:13039（DOI，題為 "Biparental inheritance of mitochondrial DNA in humans"）報告三個無關家族在多代中表現出 biparental mtDNA inheritance，即父系 mtDNA 顯著貢獻於後代（heteroplasmy 高達 ~24–76%）。論文引發強烈爭議：(1) 支持方 認為部分家族可能帶有未知的 mtDNA 父系排除機制突變；(2) 批評方（Lutz-Bonengel & Parson, PNAS 116:1821, 2019, DOI；Salas et al., BMJ Open 10:e037467, 2020）指出更可能的解釋是核-粒線體假基因（NUMTs，整合至核基因組的 mtDNA 片段）在 NGS 上產生人為的「父系 mtDNA」訊號；(3) 後續研究（Vissing 2019, Annu Rev Genomics Hum Genet 20:451, DOI）認為真實的人類父系滲漏即使存在也極為罕見。教學中應保留「母系遺傳是法則」，但補充：父系排除涉及精子粒線體標記 ubiquitin → autophagy（Sutovsky et al., Nature 402:371, 1999），偶有失效；NUMT 是 mtDNA 分析的重大陷阱。

The genetics chapter teaches strict maternal inheritance of mitochondrial DNA, which underpins mitochondrial Eve and maternal-lineage studies. This holds overwhelmingly but not absolutely. Luo et al. 2018, PNAS 115:13039 (DOI, "Biparental inheritance of mitochondrial DNA in humans") reported three unrelated families with multi-generational biparental mtDNA inheritance (paternal heteroplasmy reaching ~24–76%). The paper triggered strong debate: (1) proponents suggest these families may carry undiscovered mutations in the paternal-mtDNA-exclusion machinery; (2) critics (Lutz-Bonengel & Parson, PNAS 116:1821, 2019, DOI; Salas et al., BMJ Open 10:e037467, 2020) argue the more parsimonious explanation is nuclear-mitochondrial pseudogenes (NUMTs, mtDNA fragments integrated into the nuclear genome) producing artefactual "paternal mtDNA" reads in NGS; (3) follow-up work (Vissing 2019, Annu Rev Genomics Hum Genet 20:451, DOI) concludes that genuine human paternal leakage, if it exists, is vanishingly rare. Keep maternal inheritance as the rule but add: paternal exclusion involves sperm-mitochondrial ubiquitination → autophagy (Sutovsky et al., Nature 402:371, 1999) and can occasionally fail, and NUMTs are a major pitfall in any mtDNA analysis.

端粒長度 ≠ 老化生物標記 (Boonekamp 2013 Aging Cell)

教學在 chromatin / cell-cycle 章節提到 Hayflick limit 與 telomere 縮短時，常以「端粒長度 = 老化速率」的線性敘述帶過，需補充批判：Boonekamp et al. 2013 Aging Cell「Telomere length behaves as biomarker of somatic redundancy rather than biological age」對 124 篇人類研究做 meta-analysis，發現 telomere length 與 chronological age 的 within-individual 相關性 r ≈ −0.3，但與 mortality / disease risk 的關聯僅在調整 baseline length後才出現。Sanders & Newman 2013 Epidemiol Rev 進一步指出 leukocyte telomere length 的測量精度（qPCR vs TRF）足以解釋多數研究分歧。實務原則：(1) 引用端粒研究時必須註明測量方法（qPCR / TRF / STELA / TeSLA）；(2) 不可由單次測量推斷 biological age；(3) 縱貫 (longitudinal) 設計才能評估端粒縮短速率。

When the chromatin / cell-cycle chapter introduces Hayflick limit and telomere shortening, it often presents the linear claim that 'telomere length = aging rate'. Add criticism: Boonekamp et al. 2013 Aging Cell ('Telomere length behaves as biomarker of somatic redundancy rather than biological age') meta-analyses 124 human studies and finds telomere length correlates with chronological age within-individual at r ≈ −0.3, but its association with mortality/disease only emerges after adjusting for baseline length. Sanders & Newman 2013 Epidemiol Rev further shows measurement-platform precision (qPCR vs TRF) explains most cross-study disagreement. Rules: (1) always state the measurement method (qPCR / TRF / STELA / TeSLA); (2) a single timepoint cannot infer biological age; (3) longitudinal designs are required to estimate shortening rate.

CRISPR 編輯效率隨基因組位置變異 (Doench 2016 Nat Biotechnol)

教學在分子生物學工具章節介紹 CRISPR-Cas9 時，常以「指定 20-nt sgRNA 即可精準編輯」概述，需補充：Doench et al. 2016 Nat Biotechnol「Optimized sgRNA design to maximize activity and minimize off-target effects of CRISPR-Cas9」對 ~1 000 sgRNAs 的 saturation screen 顯示，編輯效率受 (a) chromatin accessibility (open chromatin > 5× 高效於 closed)、(b) GC content 40-60% 最佳、(c) seed region (PAM-proximal 12 nt) 序列匹配嚴格度等多因子調控。Hsu et al. 2013 Nat Biotechnol 同步指出 off-target 主要由 seed region mismatch 數量決定 (≤ 3 mismatches 高風險)。實務原則：(1) 設計 sgRNA 必須以 Rule Set 2 (Doench) 或 DeepCRISPR 等模型預測效率；(2) 至少設計 3-4 條 sgRNA 並驗證；(3) 預測 off-target 用 Cas-OFFinder / CRISPOR (Concordet 2018 NAR) 並 Sanger 確認 top-10。

When the molecular-biology tools chapter introduces CRISPR-Cas9 as 'specify a 20-nt sgRNA for precise editing', add nuance: Doench et al. 2016 Nat Biotechnol ('Optimized sgRNA design to maximize activity and minimize off-target effects of CRISPR-Cas9') saturation-screened ~1,000 sgRNAs and showed editing efficiency depends on (a) chromatin accessibility (open chromatin >5× more efficient than closed), (b) GC content 40-60% optimal, (c) strict seed-region (12 nt PAM-proximal) sequence match. Hsu et al. 2013 Nat Biotechnol further showed off-targets are dominated by seed-region mismatch count (≤3 mismatches = high risk). Rules: (1) score sgRNAs with Rule Set 2 (Doench) or DeepCRISPR; (2) design and validate ≥3-4 sgRNAs; (3) predict off-targets with Cas-OFFinder / CRISPOR (Concordet 2018 NAR) and Sanger-confirm the top-10.
