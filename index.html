生物資訊學的生物學基礎概論

從分子、細胞到組學系統——18 章雙軌教程，為 CS/統計/工程背景的讀者建立扎實的生物學底子，並引導具備生物背景者銜接到資料分析。

From molecules to cells to omics — an 18-chapter dual-track tutorial that builds rigorous biology foundations for CS/stats/engineering backgrounds, and bridges traditional biologists to data analysis.
